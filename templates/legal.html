1. Description of Service

The Service is an administrative research tool that gathers, indexes, audits, and formats public titles and metadata of administrative issuances from official Philippine national government department websites:

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026 | Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Commitment to Privacy: We adhere to the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

The Philippine Government Issuance Title Collector ("Service", "we", "us", or "our"), maintained by the Project Operations Team ("Operator"), respects your privacy. This policy outlines how information is collected, processed, and secured when accessing our administrative service.

1. Scope of Data Processing

1.1 Public Government Records (Non-Personal)

The Service indexes public administrative records from official Philippine national government portals (DA, DepEd, DOH, DENR). Under Section 4(c) of Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act does not apply to information relating to functions of public government officers. Gathered titles, issuance numbers, signing dates, and source URLs are non-personal, public domain records.

1.2 Administrative Security and Session Data

We collect only minimal technical information necessary for system operation and security:

  • Access PIN: Submitted during authentication; evaluated with cryptographic constant-time comparison. Never stored with individual identities.
  • Session Cookie (session_access_token): A secure, random UUID cookie issued upon successful verification to track the single active session. Contains no personal information.
  • Client IP Address: Processed in volatile memory solely to enforce rate limiting (10 attempts per 60 seconds). Automatically purged and never persisted to the permanent database.
  • Operational Server Logs: Standard request logs (timestamp, route, status code) for maintenance and error diagnosis.

2. Data Processing Inventory

Data Category Purpose Legal Basis (RA 10173) Retention Period
Session Cookie Single active session enforcement and access control Legitimate security interest Expires on logout or when superseded
Client IP Address Brute-force attack and flood protection Legitimate security interest 60-second sliding memory window
Public Government Records Indexing and compiling public legal metadata Public interest and legal research Retained in database until administrative reset

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application utilizes only strictly necessary technical cookies:

  • Cookie: session_access_token (First-party, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax).

We do not use third-party tracking pixels, marketing cookies, Google AdSense, or advertising networks.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information. Technical data is processed only by essential infrastructure providers:

  • PostgreSQL / Supabase: Relational database hosting for public government issuance metadata (Supabase Privacy).
  • Hosting Platform (Vercel / Render / Self-Hosted): Server runtime hosting and network routing.

5. Security Safeguards

We implement technical and organizational controls to protect data:

  • Cryptographic constant-time comparison for PIN verification (mitigating timing attacks);
  • Secure 128-bit random UUID session tokens;
  • Automatic session superseding to restrict concurrent access;
  • Rate limiting stored strictly in volatile server memory.

6. Data Subject Rights (RA 10173)

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, individuals hold rights including the Right to be Informed, Right to Access, Right to Rectification, and Right to Erasure. To submit an inquiry, please contact our Data Protection Officer.
